Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with Metmofabric.

How quickly can I get a quote?+
We aim to respond to all quote requests within 24 hours on business days. For urgent requirements, please call us directly or send a WhatsApp message and we'll prioritise your enquiry.
What information do I need to provide for a quote?+
The more detail the better. Ideally: part drawings or 3D files (STEP/IGES), required material, annual production volume, number of cavities required, and delivery timeline. We can also quote based on a part sample or verbal description as a starting point.
What is your typical lead time for injection mould manufacturing?+
Lead times vary by mould complexity. Simple single-cavity moulds typically take 4–6 weeks. Complex multi-cavity or hot runner moulds may require 8–16 weeks. We'll provide a specific timeline with your quotation.
Do you work with clients outside Ghana?+
Yes — we work with clients across West Africa and are experienced in managing remote projects. We can conduct consultations via video call, share detailed design reviews digitally, and arrange delivery across the region.
What is your payment structure?+
We typically work on a 50% deposit at project confirmation and 50% on completion before delivery. For larger projects, we can discuss milestone-based payment schedules. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
Do you offer mould maintenance contracts?+
Yes — we offer annual mould maintenance contracts that include periodic inspections, preventative servicing, and priority repair turnaround. These contracts help extend tool life and reduce unplanned production downtime.
