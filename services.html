Our Process

How We Deliver Your Project

01

Initial Consultation & Brief

▼
We begin every project with a thorough consultation — understanding your mould requirements, part geometry, material preferences, production volumes, and delivery timeline. This brief forms the foundation of your project plan.
02

Engineering Design & DFM Review

▼
Our engineering team creates precise CAD designs and performs a Design for Manufacturability review to ensure your mould will perform reliably in production. We present the design for client approval before any machining begins.
03

Quotation & Project Plan

▼
We provide a detailed, itemised quotation covering all materials, machining, and delivery costs — along with a clear project timeline with milestones. No surprises, no hidden costs.
04

Prototype & Validation

▼
For new tooling projects, we produce a prototype mould or sample part for dimensional validation and client approval. Any required adjustments are made before full production commences.
05

Full Production Manufacturing

▼
CNC machining, EDM, polishing, and assembly — with in-process quality checks at every stage. Our quality control team inspects against your specifications throughout production.
06

Final Inspection & Delivery

▼
Every completed mould undergoes a final full dimensional inspection before dispatch, with a comprehensive quality report provided. We ensure your mould arrives safely, on time, and ready to run.
