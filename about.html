Our Story

Precision Engineering is What We Do

Metmofabric Engineering Services was established with a single, clear vision: to provide West Africa with world-class precision mould manufacturing that rival any international standard.

Under the leadership of Mr. Clement N. C. Clottey, the company has grown from a specialist mould-making workshop into a comprehensive engineering solutions provider — serving clients across manufacturing, FMCG, automotive, and consumer goods sectors.

Our philosophy is simple: every mould we manufacture represents a client's investment in their production future. We treat that responsibility with the highest level of care, precision, and professionalism.

Our Leadership

Meet Our Founder & MD

CC
Mr. Clement N. C. Clottey
Founder & Managing Director — Metmofabric Engineering Services

Mr. Clement N. C. Clottey is the visionary founder and Managing Director of Metmofabric Engineering Services. With over a decade of hands-on experience in precision engineering and mould manufacturing, he has built Metmofabric into a recognised name in West Africa's industrial manufacturing landscape.

His deep technical expertise — spanning mould design, CNC machining, quality management, and production engineering — combined with a passionate commitment to client satisfaction has been the driving force behind the company's growth and reputation.

Under his leadership, Metmofabric has delivered over 500 precision moulds to clients across multiple industries, consistently earning recognition for quality, reliability, and engineering innovation.
